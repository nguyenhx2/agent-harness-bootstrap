The introduction ~164s, narrated

The questions teams putting agents on real work keep asking, each introduced, asked, and then answered by the mechanism that handles it: a team that fits the project, safety without overthinking or burning tokens, keys that stay secret, an agentic loop with ceremony matched to the change, specs a human can confirm and an AI can read, seeing and controlling how the agents work, and harness-view turning a vague score into a worklist with one-click fixes and a published rubric. Voiced, with a music bed, and captioned for muted playback.

In depth

Tailoring, skills and the viewer ~44s

What the introduction leaves out. The operating flow end to end, then a roster derived from the contract and the modules that exist, rather than every agent and a hundred skills installed before anyone read the code. Then the mechanisms that keep it honest, each with the problem it solves: Skill Discovery reads this project's manifests to find skills that match its stack, Skill Wire connects a chosen skill to the agent seat that needs it, and harness-view answers whether any of it is actually wired right.
